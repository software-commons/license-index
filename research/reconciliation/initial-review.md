## MIT and Apache-2.0

- MIT's broad permission wording and warranty disclaimer leave implied patent rights unresolved. Its clause requires both the copyright notice and the permission notice in all copies or substantial portions. The renderer changes only the designated placeholder notice line and rejects the SPDX matching template. [OSI MIT text](https://opensource.org/license/mit)
- Apache §3 terminates only the stated patent license for the work on filing qualifying patent litigation, including a cross-claim or counterclaim. The initial comparison used a separate patent-termination field and misleadingly left the common termination row unsettled. The cited profile now uses the common termination dimension and states the actual trigger. [Apache License §3](https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0.txt)
- Apache §4(d) conditions the readable attribution obligation on an upstream NOTICE and redistribution of a derivative work. Section 4 lists additional conditions independently. ASF project rules and application guidance for outside projects have different scopes. The installer preserves the publisher's Appendix and its sample fields verbatim and explains that the copy is one part of application. [Apache License §4](https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0.txt), [ASF application guidance](https://www.apache.org/legal/apply-license.html)
