# LPPL and Apache distribution provisions

Five pairs of LPPL claim revisions cover the publisher headers permitting verbatim copies of each license document and a distribution provision specific to the edition. The LPPL 1.0 finding concerns distribution of all files in the unchanged Program, including documentation when present in the original distribution. The 1.1 and 1.2 findings concern complete unmodified Program distributions. The 1.3a and 1.3c findings concern clause 6(d) when a non-Current Maintainer distributes a Derived Work subject to that provision.

The four Apache 1.1 example claims cover the conditional source and binary grant, redistribution notices, the acknowledgment and its permitted alternative, and the restriction on endorsement or promotion under the specified names. Each finding preserves the qualifications in its exact claim record.

The selected verbatim-document provisions cover the headers of the five identified LPPL publisher texts. Qualification of complete distributable artifacts and installation procedures remains pending.
