# Historical Mozilla and Netscape provisions

Six selected claims cover the initial developer grants, source-code notice requirements and source availability provisions in the Mozilla Public License 1.0 and 1.1. Each claim identifies its edition, actor, conditions and cited publisher text.

Five claims describe exceptions and specific licensing permissions for Netscape under the 1.0 and 1.1 amendments. These include contractual limits concerning third-party code, provisions for specified non-Branded products in the stated two-year period, and the 1.1 alternative-licensing provision for Netscape Branded Code. These findings describe the captured historical license provisions. Application to any particular codebase requires its own evidence.
