# Eclipse Public License qualification

Separate research and independent Codex review supported eight exact assertions about Eclipse Public License 1.0 and 2.0. Both executions reopened the publisher documents and the separate SPDX License List 3.29.0 records. The broader Eclipse profiles remain provisional.

| Assertions | Original inputs | Challenge |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Two edition identities | Eclipse Foundation English plaintext headings | Compare both edition headings and keep SPDX identity distinct from publisher project policy. |
| Two SPDX correspondences | Exact generated JSON-LD records at the pinned SPDX commit | Check each root license node, version, deprecation flag and Eclipse link. Keep matching templates and byte equivalence outside the scope. |
| Two unchanged document copy permissions | Section 7 of each official agreement | Compare exact agreement copies, an edited agreement, and distribution of the Program. |
| EPL 1.0 object agreement | Section 3, including paragraph (b)(iv) | Check whether separate object terms disclose both source availability from the distributing Contributor and reasonable access by customary means. Read the separate source-form conditions. |
| EPL 2.0 Secondary License route | Sections 1 and 3.2 and Exhibit A | Compare a valid separate-file combination and an initial Contributor's notice with a copied blank exhibit, a combination without the notice, and a notice without the combination. |

The publisher files match the existing offline installer inputs byte for byte. The composite SPDX responses are in controlled research custody. These assertions leave project licensing, general compatibility, and an assessment of any complete distribution open for separate research.
