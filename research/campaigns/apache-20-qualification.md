Six assertions about the January 2004 edition passed original-source research and separate independent verification. Each execution acquired the exact public claim revisions, opened the originals, and recorded its findings and adverse checks. The claim dispositions are `independently-reviewed`. The broader catalog profile remains provisional.

| --- | --- | --- |

| Patent grant scope | ASF English plaintext, sections 1 and 3 | Test a contributor's unrelated licensable patent and a claim requiring the Contribution combined with the submitted Work. |
| Patent litigation termination | ASF English plaintext, sections 2 and 3 | Compare a qualifying cross-claim about an incorporated Contribution with litigation over unrelated software. Check the affected grants and filing date. |
| NOTICE attribution | ASF English plaintext, sections 1 and 4(d) | Compare a Work distributed without NOTICE with a distributed Derivative Work carrying relevant attributions in accompanying documentation. Check section 4(c) separately. |
| Unchanged license document reuse | ASF licensing and distribution FAQ | Compare unchanged English text, a substantive edit, and a convenience translation. |
