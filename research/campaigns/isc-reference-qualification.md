For a licensing guide, assume the guide reproduces the displayed license body as an exact reference. The text reuse statement supports that reproduction. If the guide also ships software under the displayed ISC terms, the software copies need their own required notices. Specific software, historical variant equivalence, patent rights and a reproducible offline text format remain unresolved.
