### Illustrative assumptions

- **An assembled database includes third-party entries.** Assume the maintainer controls certain rights in the assembly and another party controls rights in some entries. Identify what the maintainer dedicated. Determine separately whether the intended use needs clearance of the other party’s rights. [Waiver scope](../../sces/records/claim/cc0-10-section-2-waiver-scope--1.json) · [Third-party clearance](../../sces/records/claim/cc0-10-section-4c-third-party-rights-clearance--1.json)
- **A supplier gives a separate accuracy assurance.** Assume a supplier applies CC0 and signs an agreement promising a stated level of accuracy. Read the separate agreement and applicable law to determine its effect. The CC0 wording establishes the terms of its as-is offer.
