# Commission English references for EUPL 1.0 and 1.1

The Index admits the EUPL 1.0 and 1.1 English editions displayed by the Commission at the URLs named in the editorial decision. Each edition has a distinct identity and a documentary correspondence to its SPDX 3.29.0 entry.

Research and independent review inspected the rendered documents, including their edition labels and distinguishing passages. The independent executions support the visible references. The captured PDF files have a separate provenance record. These claims leave file-byte equivalence, adoption dates, applicability to a particular work, text redistribution and installation unresolved.
