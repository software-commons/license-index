## Documentary identity

Revision 1 of `gcc-14-2-runtime-exception-31-instrument-identity` examines the versioned libstdc++ manual's identification of the exception, its edition and date, and the reference to `COPYING.RUNTIME`. Its descriptive evidence comes from the publisher's manual. Challenge the candidate against confusion with the compiler's release number, the GPLv3 base text and the manual's separate documentation license. This claim uses the same prospective subject as the document-copy claim. [GCC 14.2.0 manual](https://gcc.gnu.org/onlinedocs/gcc-14.2.0/libstdc%2B%2B/manual/manual/license.html#manual.intro.status.license.gpl).

The first research and review executions supported the reading and left integrity unverifiable. A later pair separately reopened the versioned manual, checked its cited passage against the annotated release and pinned `COPYING.RUNTIME`, and recorded six passing assessments for the controlled documentary scope. The independent execution challenged mixed editions, the separate GFDL documentation terms and the need for a file-specific copyright-holder notice. The original execution logs remain in local custody. The registry preserves both the initial unresolved disposition and the later independently reviewed result. The rendered inspections address documentary identity.
