The retained discovery leads for PolyForm Perimeter 1.0.1 and PolyForm Shield 1.0.0 each bind to their own named edition. The Perimeter Noncompete clause concerns providing to others a competing product. Its Competition clause expressly treats a product as competing if the actor uses the covered software to market that product as a substitute for the software's functionality or value. The Shield language also refers to a competing product that the licensor or an affiliate provides using the software. Shield has separate provisions for new products, discontinued products and sales of business. The recorded conditions leave individual products and enforceability to separate assessment. [Perimeter governing terms](https://polyformproject.org/licenses/perimeter/1.0.1) · [Shield governing terms](https://polyformproject.org/licenses/shield/1.0.0)

The Notices clause in each edition requires the person passing a copy of any part of software to supply the terms or their stated URL, together with any Required Notice lines that the licensor supplied. When evaluating a developer scenario, first establish the exact grant and check the notices provided with that particular software.
