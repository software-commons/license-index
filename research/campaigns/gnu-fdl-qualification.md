# GNU Free Documentation License qualification

Independent Codex executions supported 31 claims across editions 1.1, 1.2, and 1.3. They cover each edition’s identity and exact publisher document’s verbatim-copy permission, six SPDX 3.29.0 crosswalks per edition, and seven scoped terms. The generic identifiers leave Invariant Sections, Front-Cover Texts, and Back-Cover Texts unspecified. The `-invariants-` identifiers indicate that at least one of those features is present. The `-no-invariants-` identifiers specify that all three are absent. Each feature category has distinct `-only` and `-or-later` entries. Use the ListedLicense identifier fields for spelling because the SPDX prose notes list some suffixes in a different order. [GNU FDL 1.1](https://ftp.gnu.org/pub/gnu/Licenses/fdl-1.1.txt) · [GNU FDL 1.2](https://ftp.gnu.org/pub/gnu/Licenses/fdl-1.2.txt) · [GNU FDL 1.3](https://ftp.gnu.org/pub/gnu/Licenses/fdl-1.3.txt) · [SPDX 3.29.0 primary source](https://github.com/spdx/license-list-data/tree/31ba1a50e5397e00a304dbadc76531740e89ee48/SPDXv3/v3jsonld)

The publisher’s permission covers verbatim copies of the license document. A particular work’s authorized grant and notices determine the actual edition choice, Invariant Sections, and required Cover Texts. The Addendum gives an example notice. All 18 supported FDL identifiers were exercised offline and produced exact publisher bytes for the selected edition. The CLI preserved existing files, rejected substitutions, and created no files in dry runs.

Seven verified terms distinguish the Cover Text triggers and overflow guidance across the three editions, the GNU FDL 1.3 Opaque-copy rule, two distinct §9 reinstatement routes, and the §10 proxy provision. The first five affected revisions remain superseded. Their verified successors preserve the difference between a required Cover Text condition and placement guidance, the particular copyright holder’s first-notice condition under §9, and the Document’s authorized proxy designation. The remaining profile interpretations and project-specific scenarios are provisional.
