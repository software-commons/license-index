# Public-data documentary identity

This stage starts from the [frozen 840-record discovery population](identity-campaign-baseline.json). It resolves 12 identity obligations and 12 corresponding SPDX mappings without changing the discovery population. The admitted identifiers are `DL-DE-BY-2.0`, `DL-DE-ZERO-2.0`, `NLOD-1.0`, `NLOD-2.0`, `ODC-By-1.0`, `ODbL-1.0`, `OGL-Canada-2.0`, `OGL-UK-1.0`, `OGL-UK-2.0`, `OGL-UK-3.0`, `PDDL-1.0`, `etalab-2.0`.

Each admitted reference has a scoped publisher identity and a separate crosswalk to the exact annotated SPDX 3.29.0 generated-data release. The Index applies its own taxonomy and lifecycle. The source records identify the exact observed representation. Raw publisher captures remain in controlled custody. Application to a particular work, reuse rights, installation and representations outside the verified claims remain separate obligations.

A recorded independent review is distinct from inclusion in the canonical release selection. An unresolved registry disposition can coexist with prior research. The target population, scoped publisher investigation, selected identities, explanatory depth and artifacts are tracked separately.
