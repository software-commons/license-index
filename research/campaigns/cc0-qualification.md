# CC0 1.0 Universal qualification

Two separate Codex executions acquired the same five original sources, assessed all eight claim bindings, and challenged plausible alternative readings. The four exact claim revisions are eligible for SCES selection. The current selection covers the OSI MIT reference form. CC0's canonical admission remains pending a future selection revision.

| Subject | Qualified scope |
| --- | --- |
| Edition identity | Creative Commons identifies the named English legal instrument as CC0 1.0 Universal. The registry's proposed license kind and lifecycle state classify the record. [Identity claim](../../sces/records/claim/cc0-10-identity--1.json) |
| SPDX correspondence | The pinned SPDX 3.29.0 details record identifies `CC0-1.0` and refers to the publisher's legal code. The assessment compares the pinned source XML with the generated details and concerns the documented relationship at that release. [Crosswalk claim](../../sces/records/claim/cc0-10-spdx-3290-correspondence--1.json) |
| Patent and trademark rights | The review checked section 4(a) against both the waiver and fallback provisions. The claim concerns the instrument's express treatment of rights held by the Affirmer. [Section 4(a) claim](../../sces/records/claim/cc0-10-section-4a-unaffected-rights--1.json) |
| Publisher plaintext reuse | The pinned legal-tools repository supplies the copyright basis for redistributing the exact English plaintext. Creative Commons reserves trademark and logo rights separately. [Reuse claim](../../sces/records/claim/cc0-10-publisher-text-copy--1.json) |

The survey also matched the publisher's live and pinned plaintext and the SPDX 3.29.0 generated plaintext to the 7,048-byte bundled file. In the Windows offline test, the existing CLI recipe wrote those exact bytes and refused a second write to the same path. The survey and CLI observations remain outside the four scoped claim revisions. The broader provisional profile still contains open questions about the waiver, fallback, third-party rights, and project-specific applicability.
