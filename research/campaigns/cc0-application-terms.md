# CC0 1.0 Universal application terms

The four claim revisions have separate research and independent-review records. Both executions read the publisher's exact English plaintext and challenged the applicability of the cited clauses. The claims address the text of the instrument. Application to particular rights, uses, and legal systems remains subject to separate research. [Publisher legal code](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/legalcode.en)

| Provision | Reviewed scope |
| --- | --- |

| Section 3, fallback grant | The grant addresses affected persons and the extent of a waiver judged ineffective. Its terms distinguish a direct grant from a transfer or sublicense from another person. [Fallback grant claim](../../sces/records/claim/cc0-10-section-3-fallback-trigger-and-recipients--1.json) |
